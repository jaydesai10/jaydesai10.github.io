I am a Ph.D. candidate at the Yang Institute for Theoretical Physics (YITP) in the Department of Physics and Astronomy at Stony Brook University. My Ph.D. thesis research, with advisors Concha Gonzalez-Garcia and George Sterman, is focused mainly on high energy particle physics/particle phenomenology.

I have worked on exploring how a model-independent Standard Model Effective Field Theory (SMEFT) approach can help us better understand physics beyond the standard model. I am also working on understanding Higgs and electroweak jets at a potential muon collider, with George Sterman. My publications are available at my Inspire and Google Scholar.
